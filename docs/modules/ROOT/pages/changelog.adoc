== v1.1.0 (2026-10-15)

=== What's changed in 1.1.0

* EAF: new layered structure. Each speaker has the tiers `ref@<speaker>` (time-aligned transcription units, value = `tu_id`) and `tx@<speaker>` (enriched transcription, the only text read back). The derived word-level tiers are no longer in the files (they are generated on request). The files are valid against the official EAFv3.0 schema.
* Stable ids: unit and token ids no longer change when an `.eaf` is edited and synchronized back to the `tsv/` file. Annotations that the `.eaf` cannot carry (lemma, POS, `Language=` labels) are kept.
* Unidentified speakers: placeholders made only of question marks (`??`, `???`, ...) in order of first appearance in each conversation, each with a row in `metadata/participants.tsv`; empty `form` values are written as `_`.
* Inhalations: the isolated `h` tokens are Jefferson inhalations (`.h`). The text is restored to `.h` and the token `type` is `nonverbalbehavior` instead of `linguistic` (`nonverbal_tokens.inhalation` in the module configuration); two linguistic `h` (BZA5006 unit 506, BZA5005 unit 188) were checked individually.
* Corrected unit texts: stray symbols attached to the neighbouring word, glued words and punctuation inside words fixed.
* Automatic validation of the tables against the CSVW schema on every push.

== v1.0.0 (2026-10-11)

=== First release of ParlaBZ

ParlaBZ collects spoken Italian from South Tyrol (Alto Adige), recorded between 2020 and 2022 in the project "Interazioni simmetriche e asimmetriche: un corpus di italiano parlato in Alto Adige" (PI: D. Veronesi, Free University of Bozen-Bolzano).

* 10 conversations (about 5 hours): 8 semi-structured interviews and 2 free conversations (mealtime), with 15 speakers
* Transcriptions in the KIParla enriched notation, anonymized, with time-aligned ELAN files
* Verticalized `tsv/` files (20 columns, including the `code_variation` features), linearized `linear-enriched/` and `linear-orthographic/` texts, and CSVW metadata (`csv-metadata.json`)
* Speaker and conversation metadata in `metadata/`
